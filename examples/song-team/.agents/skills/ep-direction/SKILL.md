---
name: ep-direction
description: Develop a coherent EP through shared notes and parallel specialists.
---

1. Maintain the EP thesis, sound, visual language, constraints, and decisions in shared notes.
2. Keep undelegated song goals in work-items.
3. Split active songs into targeted TODOs for independent specialties.
4. Let specialists work concurrently.
5. Require full deliverables in review handoffs.
6. Update team notes after artist decisions.
