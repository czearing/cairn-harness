# Dinner Recipe Brief

## Dish

Ember-style roasted chicken thighs with blistered summer vegetables and
preserved lemon pan sauce.

Dessert: olive oil strawberry shortcake, designed for advance preparation and
quick assembly after dinner.

## Direction

Create an approachable dinner for 4 that expresses the restaurant's
live-fire and preserved-citrus specialty using a conventional oven and
stovetop. The chicken must have crisp skin and juicy meat, the vegetables must
provide color and freshness, and the pan sauce must be bright rather than
heavy.

## Constraints

- Total elapsed time must not exceed 75 minutes.
- Use ingredients available at a well-stocked supermarket.
- Provide exact quantities, temperatures, timing, and doneness cues.
- Keep active cooking manageable for one home cook.
- Include allergens and a dairy-free path.
- Keep dessert moderately rich and ready without disrupting hot dinner pickup.
- Do not require a grill, smoker, specialty preservation equipment, or
  restaurant-only ingredients.

## Acceptance Criteria

- Yield is 4 dinner portions.
- Chicken reaches a safe internal temperature without drying out.
- Skin remains crisp after saucing.
- Acid, richness, char, and freshness are balanced.
- Every ingredient appears in the method.
- The recipe includes make-ahead guidance, substitutions, and storage notes.
- Dessert serves 4, can be mostly prepared ahead, and includes a dairy-free path.
