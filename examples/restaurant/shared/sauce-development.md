# Sauce Development

## Operating Principles

- Smoke stays in one component per dish and is balanced by fresh acid, herbs, or clean broth.
- Preserved citrus is measured as both acid and salt. Final salinity is adjusted only after it is added.
- Oils and fresh herb finishes are held cold. Broths and jus are held hot only for the active service window.
- Shared components are marked below. Their final seasoning is changed at pickup so cross-utilization does not flatten the menu.

## Shared Production Bases

1. **Preserved citrus brine:** Meyer lemon or mandarin peel 100%, kosher salt 12%, sugar 4% by peel weight; cure 14 days, then refrigerate. Hold 30 days at 38 F or below. Use cold. Allergens: none. Dairy-free as written.
2. **Grilled cucumber water:** grilled cucumber juice 1000 g, rice vinegar 60 g, salt 8 g; grill only one cut face, juice, strain, and season. Hold 24 hours at 38 F or below. Use at 38 to 45 F. Allergens: none. Dairy-free as written.
3. **Green herb oil:** neutral oil 500 g, blanched mixed herbs 125 g; blend at 160 F for 3 minutes, strain, and chill immediately. Hold 3 days at 38 F or below. Use at 45 to 70 F. Allergens: none. Dairy-free as written.
4. **White bean miso base:** cooked cannellini 500 g, bean liquor 250 g, white miso 75 g, olive oil 60 g, cider vinegar 20 g; blend hot and pass. Hold 3 days at 38 F or below. Reheat once. Allergens: soy. Dairy-free as written.
5. **Roasted poultry jus base:** roasted poultry stock 2000 g, dry vermouth 250 g, roasted shallot 150 g; reduce to 900 g and strain. Hold 3 days at 38 F or below, or freeze 30 days. Use at 150 to 160 F. Allergens: sulfites from vermouth. Dairy-free as written.

## Tasting Menu, Courses 1 Through 6

### 1. Pacific Oyster | Grilled Cucumber, Preserved Meyer Lemon, Sea Herbs

**Preserved Meyer lemon mignonette**

- Formula: grilled cucumber water 200 g, Meyer lemon brine 24 g, champagne vinegar 18 g, minced cured peel 12 g, shallot 8 g.
- Technique: steep shallot in vinegar 10 minutes, then fold in the remaining ingredients without aerating.
- Hold and serve: 24 hours at 38 F or below; serve 38 to 45 F, 8 g per oyster.
- Allergens and adaptation: none; dairy-free as written.

**Sea herb oil**

- Formula: green herb oil 100 g, sea lettuce 4 g, chive 6 g.
- Technique: blend cold and strain through a coarse filter.
- Hold and serve: 24 hours at 38 F or below; serve cool, 3 to 4 drops per oyster.
- Allergens and adaptation: none; dairy-free as written.

**Distinct finish:** The tasting version is cucumber-led and minimally allium-heavy, with only drops of oil. This keeps the first bite saline and clean.

### 2. Ember-Baked Beet | Smoked Yogurt, Pistachio, Blood Orange

**Lightly smoked yogurt**

- Formula: whole-milk Greek yogurt 500 g, olive oil 35 g, lemon juice 18 g, salt 5 g.
- Technique: cold-smoke the yogurt for 6 minutes maximum, whisk with the remaining ingredients, and pass.
- Hold and serve: 48 hours at 38 F or below; serve 50 to 55 F.
- Allergens and adaptation: milk. For dairy-free service, replace yogurt with unsweetened cultured cashew yogurt and flag tree nuts; if avoiding a new nut allergen, use cultured sunflower cream.

**Blood orange agrodolce**

- Formula: blood orange juice 500 g, red wine vinegar 70 g, sugar 45 g, beet trim 100 g; reduce to 300 g, strain, then add fresh blood orange juice 60 g.
- Technique: reduce the first four ingredients gently, chill, and add fresh juice to restore brightness.
- Hold and serve: 4 days at 38 F or below; serve 50 to 60 F.
- Allergens and adaptation: sulfites may be present in vinegar. Dairy-free as written.

**Pistachio vinaigrette**

- Formula: toasted pistachio 100 g, olive oil 160 g, blood orange agrodolce 55 g, warm water 35 g, salt 3 g.
- Technique: crush pistachios coarsely and fold with liquids just before service.
- Hold and serve: liquids hold 3 days cold; combine with nuts for no more than 4 hours to preserve texture. Serve 60 to 70 F.
- Allergens and adaptation: pistachio. Dairy-free as written.

**Distinct finish:** Smoke is confined to the yogurt. Fresh juice in the agrodolce and crunchy pistachio prevent the beet from reading sweet or heavy.

### 3. Diver Scallop | Charred Mandarin, Fennel, Fresno Chile

**Charred mandarin and fennel vinaigrette**

- Formula: charred mandarin juice 240 g, fresh mandarin juice 120 g, rice vinegar 40 g, olive oil 150 g, toasted fennel seed 3 g, salt 4 g.
- Technique: char only half the fruit, juice, whisk with fresh juice and vinegar, then stream in oil. Add fennel seed after straining.
- Hold and serve: 2 days at 38 F or below; temper to 65 to 75 F and shake before use.
- Allergens and adaptation: none. Dairy-free as written.

**Fresno chile oil**

- Formula: neutral oil 250 g, seeded Fresno chile 75 g, sweet paprika 2 g.
- Technique: warm to 180 F for 10 minutes, blend, steep 30 minutes, and strain.
- Hold and serve: 5 days at 38 F or below; serve 65 to 75 F.
- Allergens and adaptation: none. Dairy-free as written.

**Distinct finish:** The charred mandarin is diluted one-to-one with fennel and oil on the plate, and chile oil is limited to 0.5 g per scallop. This avoids citrus dominance after the beet course.

### 4. Black Cod | Shellfish Broth, Grilled Leek, Lemon Kosho

**Clean shellfish broth**

- Formula: roasted shrimp or crab shells 1000 g, water 3000 g, dry vermouth 250 g, tomato 200 g, fennel 150 g, leek trim 150 g, kombu 15 g.
- Technique: sweat vegetables without color, add shells and vermouth, reduce by half, add water and kombu, then simmer 35 minutes only. Strain, reduce to 1800 g, and skim.
- Hold and serve: 2 days at 38 F or below, or freeze 30 days; hold during service no more than 3 hours at 150 F or above. Serve 155 to 165 F.
- Allergens and adaptation: crustacean, sulfites from vermouth. Dairy-free as written. A shellfish-free version can use roasted fish bones and kombu, but it is a separate stock, not an allergy-safe substitution from the same station.

**Lemon kosho**

- Formula: fresh Meyer lemon peel 100 g, preserved Meyer lemon peel 50 g, seeded serrano 30 g, salt 8 g, lemon juice 30 g.
- Technique: mince to a fine paste, ferment covered 3 days at cool room temperature, then refrigerate.
- Hold and serve: 21 days at 38 F or below; serve 45 to 60 F, 2 g per portion.
- Allergens and adaptation: none. Dairy-free as written.

**Grilled leek oil**

- Formula: green herb oil 150 g, lightly grilled leek greens 40 g.
- Technique: grill just to mark, cool, blend with oil, and strain.
- Hold and serve: 2 days at 38 F or below; serve 70 F.
- Allergens and adaptation: none. Dairy-free as written.

**Distinct finish:** The broth is intentionally tomato-light and unsmoked. Kosho is served as a small isolated accent, not dissolved through the broth, so the course remains savory after mandarin.

### 5. Fire-Roasted Artichoke | White Bean Miso, Nettle, Sourdough Crumb

**White bean miso cream**

- Formula: white bean miso base 500 g, artichoke cooking liquor 120 g, lemon juice 15 g.
- Technique: blend while hot, pass, and adjust with liquor to a spoon-coating consistency.
- Hold and serve: 3 days at 38 F or below; reheat once and serve 135 to 145 F.
- Allergens and adaptation: soy. Dairy-free as written.

**Nettle oil**

- Formula: neutral oil 400 g, blanched nettle 100 g, parsley 25 g.
- Technique: blend at 160 F for 3 minutes, strain, and chill immediately.
- Hold and serve: 3 days at 38 F or below; serve 65 to 75 F.
- Allergens and adaptation: none. Dairy-free as written.

**Cider acidulation**

- Formula: cider vinegar 100 g, artichoke cooking liquor 100 g, honey 20 g, salt 2 g.
- Technique: warm only enough to dissolve honey, then chill.
- Hold and serve: 7 days at 38 F or below; brush onto artichoke at pickup and serve hot.
- Allergens and adaptation: none. Dairy-free as written; replace honey with sugar for vegan service.

**Distinct finish:** The tasting puree is loosened and bright, with cider rather than preserved citrus. Nettle oil supplies freshness without repeating the sea-herb profile.

### 6. Dry-Aged Duck | Sour Cherry, Smoked Onion, Duck Jus

**Duck jus**

- Formula: roasted poultry jus base 900 g, roasted duck trim 300 g, duck stock 600 g; reduce to 750 g, then finish with sherry vinegar 18 g.
- Technique: simmer trim in the base 45 minutes, strain, reduce, defat completely, and add vinegar off heat.
- Hold and serve: 3 days at 38 F or below, or freeze 30 days; hold during service no more than 3 hours at 150 F or above. Serve 155 to 165 F.
- Allergens and adaptation: sulfites from vermouth and sherry vinegar. Dairy-free as written; do not mount with butter.

**Sour cherry gastrique**

- Formula: tart cherry juice 400 g, dried sour cherry 100 g, red wine vinegar 90 g, sugar 65 g; reduce to 350 g.
- Technique: make a light dry caramel with sugar, deglaze with vinegar and juice, add cherries, simmer 12 minutes, and chill.
- Hold and serve: 5 days at 38 F or below; serve 120 to 140 F.
- Allergens and adaptation: sulfites may be present in vinegar. Dairy-free as written.

**Smoked onion puree**

- Formula: roasted sweet onion 500 g, olive oil 65 g, water 75 g, sherry vinegar 12 g, salt 5 g.
- Technique: cold-smoke peeled roasted onion for 5 minutes maximum, then blend hot and pass.
- Hold and serve: 3 days at 38 F or below; reheat once and serve 135 to 145 F.
- Allergens and adaptation: sulfites may be present in vinegar. Dairy-free as written.

**Distinct finish:** Jus is sharp and unbuttered, cherry is portioned at 12 g, and smoke appears only in the onion. This keeps the centerpiece rich but not sticky or smoke-heavy.

## A La Carte, Dishes 1 Through 12

### 1. Pacific Oysters | Preserved Lemon Mignonette, Grilled Cucumber

**Preserved lemon mignonette**

- Formula: grilled cucumber water 200 g, Meyer lemon brine 30 g, champagne vinegar 28 g, minced cured peel 16 g, shallot 15 g, black pepper 1 g.
- Technique: steep shallot and pepper in vinegar 15 minutes, then fold in cucumber water, brine, and peel.
- Hold and serve: 24 hours at 38 F or below; serve 38 to 45 F.
- Allergens and adaptation: none. Dairy-free as written.

**Distinct finish:** Compared with tasting course 1, this version is sharper, more textured, and more allium-forward to suit multiple oysters and drinks.

### 2. Hearth Bread | Cultured Butter, Smoked Sea Salt, Citrus Ash

**Cultured butter**

- Formula: unsalted cultured butter 500 g, buttermilk 25 g, fine salt 5 g.
- Technique: paddle at 60 F until smooth, add buttermilk and salt, then shape and chill.
- Hold and serve: 4 days at 38 F or below; serve 60 to 65 F.
- Allergens and adaptation: milk. Dairy-free version: whip high-quality vegan cultured butter with oat yogurt at the same ratio; allergens depend on the commercial butter.

**Citrus ash salt**

- Formula: dried spent citrus peel 20 g, smoked sea salt 80 g, sugar 4 g.
- Technique: dry peel completely, char to black over the hearth, cool, pulverize, and blend with salt and sugar.
- Hold and serve: 14 days airtight at room temperature; serve at room temperature, 0.5 g per portion.
- Allergens and adaptation: none. Dairy-free as written.

**Risk flag, too smoky:** Smoked salt plus charred ash can dominate. Use lightly smoked salt, keep ash to 20% of the blend, and do not smoke the butter.

### 3. Crispy Rice | Dungeness Crab, Mandarin Kosho, Scallion

**Mandarin kosho emulsion**

- Formula: mayonnaise 300 g, mandarin kosho 35 g, fresh mandarin juice 30 g, rice vinegar 12 g.
- Technique: whisk cold until smooth; do not blend long enough to turn bitter.
- Hold and serve: 24 hours at 38 F or below; serve 45 to 50 F.
- Allergens and adaptation: egg; possible soy if commercial mayonnaise contains soy oil. Dairy-free as written. For egg-free service, use aquafaba mayonnaise made and stored as a separate batch.

**Mandarin kosho**

- Formula: fresh mandarin peel 100 g, preserved mandarin peel 40 g, seeded Fresno chile 25 g, salt 7 g, mandarin juice 25 g.
- Technique: mince to a fine paste, ferment covered 3 days at cool room temperature, then refrigerate.
- Hold and serve: 21 days at 38 F or below; use cold.
- Allergens and adaptation: none. Dairy-free as written.

**Scallion oil**

- Formula: neutral oil 300 g, scallion greens 100 g.
- Technique: blend at 160 F for 3 minutes, strain, and chill immediately.
- Hold and serve: 3 days at 38 F or below; serve 65 to 75 F.
- Allergens and adaptation: none. Dairy-free as written.

**Distinct finish:** This kosho is sweeter and less saline than lemon kosho. Fat from mayonnaise and scallion oil rounds the citrus so it does not resemble the raw oyster finish.

### 4. Ember-Baked Beets | Smoked Yogurt, Pistachio, Blood Orange

Use the tasting-course smoked yogurt and blood orange agrodolce formulas and limits.

**A la carte pistachio relish**

- Formula: toasted pistachio 120 g, blood orange agrodolce 70 g, minced shallot 15 g, parsley 12 g, olive oil 100 g.
- Technique: combine no more than 30 minutes before service.
- Hold and serve: hold 4 hours at 38 F or below; serve 60 to 70 F.
- Allergens and adaptation: pistachio; sulfites may be present in vinegar. Dairy-free as written.

**Distinct finish:** Increase black pepper in the yogurt to 0.2% and use a chunky, herbaceous relish rather than the tasting vinaigrette. Keep smoke duration unchanged.

### 5. Coal-Roasted Cabbage | Sunflower Tahini, Kumquat, Mint

**Sunflower tahini**

- Formula: toasted sunflower seed 300 g, water 300 g, olive oil 60 g, lemon juice 45 g, garlic 8 g, salt 6 g.
- Technique: blend seeds to a paste, stream in water, then add oil, lemon, garlic, and salt. Pass if needed.
- Hold and serve: 3 days at 38 F or below; serve 90 to 110 F.
- Allergens and adaptation: sunflower seed. Dairy-free as written.

**Kumquat relish**

- Formula: seeded kumquat 300 g, cider vinegar 80 g, sugar 60 g, water 80 g, salt 4 g; add minced fresh kumquat 75 g after cooling.
- Technique: simmer the first five ingredients 8 minutes, chill, then fold in fresh kumquat.
- Hold and serve: 5 days at 38 F or below; serve 55 to 65 F.
- Allergens and adaptation: none. Dairy-free as written.

**Mint oil**

- Formula: neutral oil 300 g, blanched mint 75 g, parsley 25 g.
- Technique: blend at 160 F for 3 minutes, strain, and chill immediately.
- Hold and serve: 2 days at 38 F or below; serve 65 to 75 F.
- Allergens and adaptation: none. Dairy-free as written.

**Distinct finish:** Creamy seed sauce buffers the bitter coal notes; fresh kumquat and mint lift the plate. Do not char the relish.

### 6. Fire-Roasted Artichoke | White Bean Miso, Nettle, Sourdough Crumb

Use the tasting-course white bean miso base, nettle oil, and cider acidulation formulas and limits.

**Distinct finish:** For a la carte, keep the bean puree thicker by reducing artichoke liquor to 60 g per 500 g base, increase cider acidulation to 10 g per portion, and add 1 g minced fresh parsley. The tasting course remains more broth-like and restrained.

**Adaptation note:** The sauce set is dairy-free. Sourdough crumb contains wheat and may contain milk depending on the bread; use toasted gluten-free seed crumb for gluten-free service.

### 7. Diver Scallops | Charred Mandarin, Fennel, Fresno Chile

Use the tasting-course charred mandarin and fennel vinaigrette and Fresno chile oil formulas and limits.

**Fennel broth**

- Formula: fennel trim 500 g, water 1500 g, dry vermouth 100 g, kombu 8 g, fennel pollen 0.5 g.
- Technique: sweat fennel without color, deglaze with vermouth, add water and kombu, simmer 25 minutes, strain, and add pollen.
- Hold and serve: 2 days at 38 F or below; hold during service no more than 3 hours at 150 F or above. Serve 145 to 155 F.
- Allergens and adaptation: sulfites from vermouth. Dairy-free as written.

**Distinct finish:** The a la carte plate uses warm fennel broth and only 12 g vinaigrette per scallop. This makes it more savory and less citrus-forward than tasting course 3.

### 8. Black Cod | Shellfish Broth, Grilled Leek, Lemon Kosho

Use the tasting-course clean shellfish broth, lemon kosho, and grilled leek oil formulas and limits.

**Distinct finish:** For a la carte, reduce the broth by an additional 10%, add grilled leek rounds, and finish each bowl with 3 g leek oil. Keep kosho at 2 g and separate from the broth.

**Risk flag, operationally fragile:** Shellfish broth quality drops with prolonged hot holding and develops bitterness if shells simmer too long. Produce in small daily batches, use 3-hour service batches, and never carry hot broth into a second service.

### 9. Whole Grilled Rockfish | Preserved Citrus, Spring Onion, Herb Broth

**Spring onion herb broth**

- Formula: light fish stock 1500 g, spring onion whites 250 g, dry vermouth 120 g, parsley stem 40 g, tarragon 15 g; finish with green herb oil 60 g and lemon juice 25 g.
- Technique: sweat onion without color, deglaze with vermouth, add stock, simmer 20 minutes, strain, and add herbs for a 5-minute infusion. Add oil and lemon at pickup.
- Hold and serve: broth base holds 2 days at 38 F or below; once herbs are infused, use within 4 hours. Serve 150 to 160 F.
- Allergens and adaptation: fish, sulfites from vermouth. Dairy-free as written.

**Preserved citrus relish**

- Formula: rinsed preserved Meyer lemon peel 80 g, fresh orange flesh 120 g, spring onion green 30 g, parsley 20 g, olive oil 100 g, champagne vinegar 15 g.
- Technique: hand-cut all solids into 1/8-inch dice and fold with oil and vinegar.
- Hold and serve: 24 hours at 38 F or below; serve 55 to 65 F.
- Allergens and adaptation: sulfites may be present in vinegar. Dairy-free as written.

**Distinct finish:** Citrus appears as a coarse, low-brine relish rather than kosho. Fresh orange and tarragon distinguish it from the oyster, scallop, and cod profiles.

### 10. Grilled Quail | Chicory, Fermented Plum, Toasted Sesame

**Fermented plum glaze**

- Formula: fermented plum puree 250 g, roasted poultry jus base 250 g, water 100 g, sherry vinegar 25 g, honey 20 g.
- Technique: simmer 8 minutes, strain, and reduce only to a light glaze. Brush quail once in the final minute of cooking.
- Hold and serve: 4 days at 38 F or below; serve 140 to 150 F.
- Allergens and adaptation: sulfites from vermouth and vinegar. Dairy-free as written; replace honey with sugar if needed.

**Sesame chicory dressing**

- Formula: toasted sesame paste 100 g, plum ferment liquid 45 g, rice vinegar 35 g, water 100 g, sesame oil 15 g.
- Technique: whisk sesame paste with liquids gradually until smooth.
- Hold and serve: 3 days at 38 F or below; serve 60 to 70 F.
- Allergens and adaptation: sesame. Dairy-free as written.

**Distinct finish:** Plum glaze is savory and thin, not barbecue-like. Bitter chicory and vinegar prevent the fruit and hearth char from becoming sweet or smoky.

### 11. Dry-Aged Duck Breast | Sour Cherry, Smoked Onion, Duck Jus

Use the tasting-course duck jus, sour cherry gastrique, and smoked onion puree formulas and limits.

**Distinct finish:** For a la carte, reduce jus 10% further, fold chopped rehydrated sour cherries into the gastrique at 15% by weight, and serve 20 g onion puree. The tasting course uses smooth gastrique and only 10 g puree.

**Risk flag, too smoky:** Do not smoke the duck, jus, or cherry. The 5-minute onion smoke is the sole smoke treatment.

### 12. Ember-Roasted Lamb | Fava Bean, Green Garlic, Preserved Lemon

**Green garlic lamb jus**

- Formula: lamb stock 1500 g, roasted lamb trim 300 g, dry vermouth 150 g, green garlic whites 120 g; reduce to 700 g and finish with lemon juice 15 g.
- Technique: sweat green garlic without color, add trim and vermouth, reduce by half, add stock, simmer 45 minutes, strain, reduce, and add lemon off heat.
- Hold and serve: 3 days at 38 F or below, or freeze 30 days; hold during service no more than 3 hours at 150 F or above. Serve 155 to 165 F.
- Allergens and adaptation: sulfites from vermouth. Dairy-free as written; do not mount with butter.

**Fava and green garlic puree**

- Formula: blanched peeled fava 500 g, green garlic oil 75 g, hot water 180 g, lemon juice 20 g, salt 6 g.
- Technique: blend hot until smooth and pass; cool quickly if made ahead.
- Hold and serve: 2 days at 38 F or below; reheat once and serve 135 to 145 F.
- Allergens and adaptation: none. Dairy-free as written.

**Preserved lemon salsa**

- Formula: rinsed preserved Meyer lemon peel 60 g, parsley 50 g, mint 20 g, green garlic tops 30 g, olive oil 140 g, red wine vinegar 20 g.
- Technique: hand-chop and fold together no more than 2 hours before service.
- Hold and serve: 4 hours at 38 F or below; serve 60 to 70 F.
- Allergens and adaptation: sulfites may be present in vinegar. Dairy-free as written.

**Distinct finish:** The salsa is herb-dominant and rinsed, so preserved lemon supplies salinity without the intensity of kosho. Green garlic links puree and jus while fresh mint separates the dish from duck.

## Menu-Level Risk Flags

- **Citrus load:** Tasting courses 1 through 4 contain lemon, orange, mandarin, and lemon kosho in sequence. Course 2 should be described and seasoned as blood orange agrodolce rather than a citrus sauce, and course 4 kosho must remain a small side accent. Even with that mitigation, this four-course run conflicts with the direction limiting preserved citrus to two adjacent tasting courses because courses 1 and 4 explicitly use preserved citrus and course 3 uses fresh citrus. The head chef should consider replacing course 2 blood orange with tart pomegranate or moving course 2.
- **Smoke load:** Tasting courses 2 and 6 and a la carte dishes 2 and 11 use smoke. All are safe if the listed short cold-smoke times are enforced and no protein, jus, or bread receives additional smoke.
- **Fragile emulsions:** Mandarin kosho mayonnaise and sunflower tahini should be produced in small batches and never frozen. Shake charred mandarin vinaigrette at pickup.
- **Fragile green finishes:** Sea herb, nettle, mint, scallion, grilled leek, and green herb oils require immediate chilling and dated squeeze bottles. Discard at the stated limit rather than combining old and new batches.
- **Hot-hold risk:** Shellfish, fennel, fish, poultry, and lamb liquids lose clarity and freshness under long holding. Split service into small batches and cap hot holding at 3 hours.
- **Allergen controls:** Shellfish broth, dairy sauces, egg emulsion, soy miso, sesame dressing, pistachio relish, sunflower tahini, fish broth, and wheat crumb require separate labeled containers and utensils. Adaptations must be made as separate batches, not by removing a garnish at the pass.
