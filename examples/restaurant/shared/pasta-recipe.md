# Ember-Roasted Tomato Tagliolini

**Dungeness crab, preserved Meyer lemon, basil**

Yield: 4 appetizer portions

## Ingredients

### Tagliolini

- 240 g tipo 00 flour
- 60 g fine semolina, plus more for dusting
- 3 large eggs
- 2 egg yolks
- 5 g extra-virgin olive oil

### Sauce and Finish

- 500 g ripe cherry tomatoes
- 30 g extra-virgin olive oil, divided
- 1 small shallot, thinly sliced
- 1 garlic clove, thinly sliced
- 120 g cooked Dungeness crab meat, picked over
- 20 g rinsed preserved Meyer lemon peel, finely diced
- 8 g fresh lemon juice
- 15 g unsalted butter
- 12 basil leaves, torn
- Kosher salt
- Freshly ground black pepper

## Method

1. Mound the flour and semolina on a work surface. Make a well and add the eggs,
   yolks, and 5 g olive oil. Mix, then knead for 8 to 10 minutes until smooth.
   Wrap and rest at room temperature for 45 minutes.
2. Roast the tomatoes in a grill basket over clean, medium embers until blistered
   and lightly charred, 4 to 6 minutes. The tomatoes should taste sweet and
   fire-kissed, not smoky. Reserve their juices.
3. Warm 15 g olive oil in a wide pan over medium-low heat. Add the shallot and
   garlic and cook without browning until tender. Add the tomatoes and their
   juices, crush lightly, and simmer for 5 minutes. Season cautiously because
   the preserved lemon and crab add salinity.
4. Roll the rested dough through a pasta machine to about 1 mm thick. Cut into
   2 mm tagliolini, dust lightly with semolina, and portion.
5. Boil the pasta in well-salted water until just shy of al dente, about 60 to
   90 seconds. Transfer it directly to the tomato pan with 120 g pasta water.
6. Toss vigorously over medium heat until the sauce coats the noodles. Fold in
   the crab, preserved lemon, lemon juice, butter, and remaining 15 g olive oil.
   Add pasta water as needed. Warm the crab gently without breaking it up.
7. Remove from the heat, fold in the basil, and adjust with salt and black
   pepper. Twirl into warm bowls and spoon the remaining crab and tomato over
   each portion.

## Service Notes

- The dish should be glossy and loose, with bright acidity and only a restrained
  ember character.
- Roast the tomatoes up to 1 day ahead. Make and cut the pasta up to 4 hours
  ahead, then refrigerate it in well-dusted nests.
- Allergens: wheat, egg, milk, shellfish.
- Dairy-free adaptation: omit the butter and finish with 10 g additional olive
  oil.
- Shellfish-free adaptation: replace crab with 160 g torn ember-roasted oyster
  mushrooms and increase the fresh lemon juice to 12 g.
