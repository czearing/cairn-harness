## Corrected One-Cook Sequence

1. Heat the oven, macerate berries, mix and bake shortcakes.
2. While shortcakes bake, cut vegetables only. Do not handle raw chicken on the
   dessert or vegetable work surface.
3. Remove shortcakes to a rack. Replace the parchment or use a clean unlined
   pan. Raise the oven temperature.
4. Pat and season chicken, then wash hands and sanitize the board, knife,
   counter, faucet, and handles touched during raw-chicken prep.
5. Toss vegetables and spread in a single layer.
6. Render chicken skin in an uncrowded skillet, turn skin-side up, then roast
   chicken and vegetables.
7. Check every thigh with an instant-read thermometer. Rest chicken uncovered
   while reducing sauce.
8. Plate sauce under vegetables and beside chicken. Keep all liquid off the
   skin.
9. Whip cream after the main course, or use cream whipped and refrigerated
   before cooking. Assemble shortcakes only when serving.

## Food Safety and Leftovers

- Cook every thigh to at least 74 C or 165 F at its thickest point without
  touching bone.
- Do not rinse raw chicken. Prevent raw poultry juices from contacting berries,
  herbs, cooked shortcakes, handles, or serving utensils.
- Refrigerate chicken, vegetables, sauce, berries, and whipped cream within
  2 hours, or within 1 hour when ambient temperature exceeds 32 C or 90 F.
- Refrigerate chicken, vegetables, and sauce separately for 3 to 4 days.
- Reheat chicken skin-side up in a 200 C or 400 F oven until it reaches 74 C or
  165 F. Reheat sauce separately and spoon it around, never over, the skin.
- Discard perishable food held beyond the limits above.
