# Coastal California Dinner for Four

## Menu

- Ember-style roasted chicken thighs
- Blistered zucchini, sweet peppers, and cherry tomatoes
- Preserved Meyer lemon and herb pan sauce
- Olive oil strawberry shortcakes

Yield: 4 dinner portions  
Elapsed time: 70 to 75 minutes  
Active time: about 45 minutes

## Equipment

- Oven
- 12-inch oven-safe skillet
- Sheet pan
- Medium mixing bowl
- Instant-read thermometer

## Ingredients

### Olive Oil Strawberry Shortcakes

- 340 g strawberries, hulled and quartered
- 35 g granulated sugar, divided
- 1 tsp fresh lemon juice
- 180 g all-purpose flour
- 2 tsp baking powder
- 1/4 tsp kosher salt
- 120 g cold heavy cream, plus 1 tbsp for brushing
- 45 g extra-virgin olive oil
- 1/2 tsp vanilla extract
- 120 g heavy cream, cold

### Chicken and Vegetables

- 4 large bone-in, skin-on chicken thighs, about 1.1 kg total
- 2 tsp kosher salt
- 1/2 tsp black pepper
- 1 tbsp extra-virgin olive oil
- 2 medium zucchini, cut into 2 cm pieces
- 2 red or yellow sweet peppers, cut into 2 cm pieces
- 250 g cherry tomatoes
- 1 small red onion, cut into 8 wedges
- 1 tsp dried oregano

### Preserved Lemon Pan Sauce

- 1 small shallot, finely chopped
- 120 ml low-sodium chicken stock
- 2 tbsp dry white wine or additional chicken stock
- 20 g preserved Meyer lemon peel, rinsed and finely chopped
- 1 tsp fresh lemon juice, plus more if needed
- 15 g cold unsalted butter
- 2 tbsp chopped flat-leaf parsley
- 1 tbsp chopped basil

## Method

### 1. Start Dessert

1. Heat the oven to 220 C or 425 F. Line a sheet pan with parchment.
2. Toss the strawberries with 15 g sugar and the lemon juice. Refrigerate until
   serving.
3. Whisk the flour, baking powder, salt, and remaining 20 g sugar in a bowl.
   Stir in 120 g cream, olive oil, and vanilla just until a soft dough forms.
4. Divide into 4 mounds on the sheet pan. Flatten each to 2 cm thick and brush
   with 1 tbsp cream.
5. Bake for 13 to 15 minutes, until golden and firm underneath. Transfer to a
   rack. Raise the oven to 230 C or 450 F.

### 2. Roast the Chicken and Vegetables

1. While the shortcakes bake, pat the chicken very dry. Season all over with
   1 1/2 tsp salt and the pepper.
2. After handling the chicken, wash hands and sanitize the board, knife,
   counter, faucet, and handles. Toss the zucchini, peppers, tomatoes, and onion
   with olive oil, oregano, and the remaining 1/2 tsp salt. Spread them in one
   layer on the sheet pan with fresh parchment after the shortcakes come out.
3. Place the chicken skin-side down in a cold oven-safe skillet. Set over
   medium heat and cook for 8 to 10 minutes, until the skin is deeply golden
   and has released enough fat to move freely. Pour off all but 1 tbsp fat.
4. Turn the thighs skin-side up. Put the skillet and vegetable pan in the oven.
   Roast for 18 to 25 minutes. Stir the vegetables once after 12 minutes.
5. Begin checking each thigh at 18 minutes. Remove each piece when the thickest
   part reaches at least 74 C or 165 F.
   For more tender thighs, target 77 to 80 C or 170 to 176 F without touching
   the bone. Rest skin-side up for 8 minutes. Roast the vegetables longer if
   needed, until browned at the edges and the tomatoes have burst.

### 3. Make the Pan Sauce

1. Transfer the rested chicken to a warm plate, keeping the skin uncovered.
2. Set the skillet over medium heat. Add the shallot and cook in the reserved
   drippings for 1 minute.
3. Add the wine and scrape up the browned bits. Add the stock and preserved
   lemon. Boil for 3 to 4 minutes, until reduced to about 120 ml and lightly
   syrupy.
4. Remove from the heat. Stir in fresh lemon juice and cold butter until glossy,
   then add parsley and basil. Taste before adding salt. Add more fresh lemon
   juice if the sauce needs brightness.

### 4. Plate Dinner

Divide the blistered vegetables among four plates. Spoon the sauce beside and
under the vegetables, reserving a little for the table. Set one chicken thigh
on each plate with the crisp skin above the sauce.

### 5. Finish Dessert

Whip the cold cream to soft peaks. Split each shortcake, spoon strawberries and
their juices over the bottom, add whipped cream, and replace the top. Assemble
only when ready to serve.

## Coordinated Timeline

- 0:00: Heat oven, macerate strawberries, and mix shortcakes.
- 0:10: Bake shortcakes. Prep chicken and vegetables.
- 0:25: Raise oven temperature. Begin rendering chicken skin.
- 0:35: Put chicken and vegetables in oven.
- 0:48: Stir vegetables. Keep dessert cream cold.
- 0:55: Check chicken temperature.
- 1:00: Rest chicken and make pan sauce.
- 1:08: Plate dinner.
- 1:10 to 1:15: Serve dinner. Assemble dessert after the main course.

## Make-Ahead, Storage, and Substitutions

- Bake shortcakes up to 8 hours ahead and keep covered at room temperature.
- Macerate berries up to 4 hours ahead and refrigerate.
- Cut vegetables and mix the sauce herbs up to 1 day ahead. Refrigerate
  separately.
- If preserved lemon is unavailable, combine 2 tsp finely grated lemon zest
  with 1/4 tsp kosher salt and let stand for 10 minutes.
- Boneless chicken thighs may be used, but begin checking after 12 minutes in
  the oven.
- Refrigerate chicken, vegetables, sauce, berries, and whipped cream within
  2 hours, or within 1 hour above 32 C or 90 F. Store chicken, vegetables, and
  sauce separately for 3 to 4 days. Reheat chicken skin-side up in a 200 C or
  400 F oven until it reaches 74 C or 165 F, and reheat sauce separately.
- Refrigerate shortcakes and berries separately for up to 2 days.

## Allergens and Adaptations

- Allergens: wheat, milk, and sulfites if using wine. Check stock and preserved
  lemon labels if needed.
- The chicken, vegetables, and unmounted sauce are gluten-free as written.
- Dairy-free dinner: replace butter in the sauce with 1 tbsp olive oil. For
  dessert, replace both measures of cream with a full-fat unsweetened oat
  culinary cream suitable for baking and whipping.
- Gluten-free dessert: use a reliable cup-for-cup gluten-free flour blend with
  xanthan gum and confirm the baking powder is gluten-free.

## Validation Notes

- Every listed ingredient is used in the method.
- The sauce is plated beneath or beside the chicken to preserve crisp skin.
- Chicken safety is verified by thermometer rather than time alone.
- Dessert work is front-loaded and does not conflict with hot pickup.
- The recipe uses normal home equipment and supermarket ingredients.
