# Menu Direction - Shared Doc (owner: head-chef)

## Restaurant Concept
Name: Ember & Olive
Specialty: Mediterranean coastal cuisine centered on wood-fire cooking and
olive oil. Live-fire grill and hearth are the anchor of the kitchen. Dishes
lean toward Southern Italy, Greece, and the Levant with a modern, produce
forward presentation.

## Core Ingredients / Pantry
- Fats: extra virgin olive oil (finishing and cooking), cultured butter
- Proteins: whole fish, lamb, octopus, chicken, seasonal game in tasting menu
- Produce: tomatoes, eggplant, zucchini, citrus, stone fruit, figs, fennel
- Grains/legumes: farro, chickpeas, lentils, semolina, phyllo
- Herbs/aromatics: oregano, thyme, mint, dill, garlic, preserved lemon, sumac
- Dairy: sheep and goat cheeses (feta, ricotta, labneh), yogurt
- Nuts: pistachio, almond, pine nut, walnut
- Sweeteners: honey, date syrup, brown sugar

## Constraints (apply to all dishes)
1. Every savory dish must use the wood-fire grill or hearth in some step
   (char, roast, smoke, or ember-cooked component) unless it is a raw/cured
   starter.
2. Olive oil is the primary fat; butter is allowed only in pastry/dessert work.
3. No em dashes in any recipe or doc text.
4. Keep proteins seasonal and avoid duplicate primary proteins across the
   a la carte list (no more than 2 dishes per primary protein).
5. Tasting menu must be 7 courses, one continuous story from raw/light to
   rich to dessert, and must not repeat any a la carte dish exactly.
6. Every dish needs: name, short description, primary components, cooking
   method, and one accompanying sauce or condiment.
7. Recipes must be nut-allergy labeled and have a vegetarian option flagged
   where applicable.

## Work Split
- sauce-chef: savory a la carte (11 dishes) + savory tasting courses
  (courses 1-6), including all accompanying sauces/condiments.
- pastry-chef: dessert a la carte (4 dishes) + tasting course 7 (dessert),
  plus any bread/dough components used across the menu.
- recipe-tester: evaluates all submitted recipes against this doc once both
  chefs deliver.

## Acceptance Criteria
- 15 a la carte dishes total (11 savory + 4 dessert), no duplicate primary
  proteins beyond the limit, each dish complete per constraint 6.
- 7-course tasting menu following the story arc in constraint 5.
- All constraints above satisfied; recipe-tester confirms before menu is
  considered final.
